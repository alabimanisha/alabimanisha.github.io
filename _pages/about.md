I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Bellini College of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Computing (CAICC), University of South Florida, jointly supervised by [Dr. Guangjing Wang](https://www.usf.edu/ai-cybersecurity-computing/people/faculty/guangjing-wang.aspx) and [Dr. Sriram Chellappan](https://www.usf.edu/ai-cybersecurity-computing/people/faculty/chellappan-sriram.aspx). My research lies at the intersection of **trustworthy and explainable vision applications**, with a focus on the security of vision models for civil systems - crowd counting, pedestrian and vehicle detection. 

Before USF, I earned an M.S. in Civil Engineering (Transportation) - Smart Cities Track at the University of Central Florida and a B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).
